The Daily Mail has launched Relink, an innovative puzzle game that combines lateral thinking with daily challenges. This engaging brainteaser presents players with a grid of words where the goal is to identify the imposters that dont belong. With a unique twist, the game not only tests your ability to spot the odd one out but also requires you to uncover the hidden connection between them, making it a true test of wit and creativity.

Relink stands out in the crowded world of puzzle games by encouraging players to think outside the box. Each day, a new puzzle is released at midnight, inviting users to return daily and keep their streak alive. The game is designed to cater to all skill levels, making it a perfect fit for casual players and puzzle enthusiasts alike. As you dive into the game, youll find that its not just about solving puzzles; its about enjoying the process of discovery and the satisfaction that comes with each solved challenge.
